# Learning and Development Policy
## NordTech AB
**Version:** 1.2
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2026
**Approved by:** People & Culture, Executive Leadership Team
**Next Review:** January 2027

---

## 1. Introduction

1.1 NordTech AB believes that continuous learning and professional growth are fundamental to individual performance, employee satisfaction, and the long-term success of the business. This policy sets out NordTech AB's commitment to developing its people and the entitlements and obligations that apply to all employees.

1.2 This policy applies to all permanent employees of NordTech AB. Fixed-term employees and contractors are eligible for mandatory training requirements only, unless their contract states otherwise.

1.3 Line managers are responsible for supporting the development of their team members, having regular learning conversations, and ensuring compliance with mandatory training requirements. People & Culture is responsible for the design and governance of the company-wide learning framework.

---

## 2. Mandatory Training

### 2.1 Overview

2.1.1 Certain training is mandatory for all employees or for specific roles at NordTech AB. Completion of mandatory training is a condition of employment and is treated as a work obligation equivalent to any other contractual duty.

2.1.2 Mandatory training requirements are set by People & Culture in consultation with Legal, Security, and relevant senior leadership. The current list of mandatory training modules is maintained on the company intranet and in SmartHRIS.

### 2.2 Categories of Mandatory Training

2.2.1 **Company-wide mandatory training** is required of all NordTech AB employees regardless of role. This includes, but is not limited to:

| Module | Frequency |
|---|---|
| Information Security Awareness | Annual |
| GDPR and Data Protection | Annual |
| Workplace Health and Safety | Annual |
| Anti-Harassment and Respectful Workplace | Annual |
| Code of Conduct and Ethics | Biennial (every 2 years) |
| Anti-Bribery and Corruption | Biennial (every 2 years) |

2.2.2 **Role-specific mandatory training** applies to employees in particular functions and is determined by the relevant department head in consultation with People & Culture. Examples include:
- Payment Card Industry (PCI-DSS) compliance for employees handling payment data.
- Financial crime and AML training for employees in Finance and Legal.
- Secure development lifecycle (SDL) training for all engineers.

2.2.3 **New joiner mandatory training** must be completed within **30 days** of an employee's start date. The required modules are outlined in the onboarding programme in SmartHRIS.

### 2.3 Completion Deadlines

2.3.1 Employees will be notified of upcoming mandatory training requirements at least **4 weeks in advance** of the completion deadline, via SmartHRIS and email.

2.3.2 All mandatory training must be completed by the stated deadline. It is the employee's responsibility to complete assigned training within working hours. Line managers must ensure that employees have sufficient protected time to complete training.

2.3.3 Completion status is tracked automatically in SmartHRIS. Reminders are sent at 2 weeks and 1 week before the deadline.

### 2.4 Consequences of Non-Completion

2.4.1 Failure to complete mandatory training by the stated deadline is treated as a work performance issue and will be addressed through the following escalation process:

| Stage | Trigger | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Deadline missed | Automated reminder; line manager notified |
| 2 | 5 working days past deadline | Line manager formal conversation; written note on record |
| 3 | 10 working days past deadline | People & Culture formal written warning |
| 4 | 15 working days past deadline | Escalation to senior leadership; potential suspension of system access pending completion |
| 5 | 20 working days past deadline | Formal disciplinary process initiated |

2.4.2 In exceptional circumstances where an employee is unable to complete mandatory training due to extended sick leave, approved long-term absence, or other extraordinary reason, a revised completion deadline will be agreed between the employee, their line manager, and People & Culture.

2.4.3 Where non-completion of compliance training poses a regulatory, legal, or reputational risk to NordTech AB, the company reserves the right to escalate directly to stage 3 or beyond without following the sequential stages outlined above.

2.4.4 Repeated failure to complete mandatory training across multiple cycles may constitute a conduct issue and may result in formal disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment.

## 3. Study Leave

### 3.1 Entitlement

3.1.1 Employees who have completed at least **1 year of continuous service** at NordTech AB are entitled to **5 days of paid study leave per calendar year** to support formal education or professional development activities.

3.1.2 Study leave is in addition to the employee's annual leave entitlement and is non-transferable between calendar years (it does not carry over).

3.1.3 Study leave may be taken in single days or in blocks, subject to business needs and manager approval.

### 3.2 Eligible Activities

3.2.1 Study leave may be used for:
- Attending lectures, seminars, or workshops as part of a formal educational programme.
- Sitting professional or vocational examinations.
- Completing assessed coursework or preparation for examinations.
- Attending a professional development bootcamp or intensive programme.

3.2.2 Study leave may not be used for:
- Self-directed online learning that can reasonably be undertaken outside working hours.
- Activities unrelated to the employee's current or foreseeable future role.
- Personal interest courses with no professional relevance.

3.2.3 In borderline cases, the line manager and People & Culture will determine eligibility in consultation with the employee.

### 3.3 Application Process

3.3.1 Study leave requests must be submitted via SmartHRIS with a minimum of **2 weeks' notice**, including:
- The name and description of the activity or programme.
- The dates and hours required.
- Evidence of enrolment or registration (e.g., course confirmation, exam booking).
- A brief explanation of relevance to the employee's role or development.

3.3.2 Approval is at the discretion of the line manager with input from People & Culture where needed. Requests will not be unreasonably refused.

## 4. Professional Certification Funding

### 4.1 Entitlement

4.1.1 NordTech AB will fund professional certifications and relevant learning activities for eligible employees up to a maximum of **5,000 SEK per employee per calendar year**.

4.1.2 This funding is available to all permanent employees who have completed their probationary period. Part-time employees receive the full allowance on the same basis as full-time employees.

4.1.3 Unused certification funding does not roll over into the following calendar year.

### 4.2 Eligible Expenditure

4.2.1 Certification funding may be used for:
- Registration fees for professional examinations (e.g., AWS, Azure, GCP, CISA, CISM, PMP, Scrum certifications).
- Purchase of official study materials (textbooks, e-learning platforms, practice exams).
- Attendance fees for professional development conferences directly relevant to the employee's role.
- Membership fees for relevant professional bodies (e.g., ISACA, IEEE, ACM).

4.2.2 Certification funding may not be used for:
- General consumer software, hardware, or equipment.
- University degree tuition fees (a separate Education Assistance scheme is available — contact People & Culture).
- Activities primarily personal in nature.
- Any activity already funded by a client, grant, or third party.

### 4.3 Application and Reimbursement

4.3.1 Employees must submit a **pre-approval request** via SmartHRIS before incurring any expenditure. Pre-approval requests require:
- Description of the certification or activity.
- Estimated cost and breakdown.
- Explanation of relevance to current or planned role.

4.3.2 Pre-approval is required for all expenditure. Expenses incurred without pre-approval will not be reimbursed.

4.3.3 Following pre-approval, expenses are claimed via the standard expense process in SmartHRIS with original receipts or digital invoices attached.

4.3.4 Where a certification requires **re-sit fees** due to a failed examination, NordTech AB will fund one re-sit per certification per calendar year on a case-by-case basis, subject to manager and People & Culture approval.

### 4.4 Repayment Clause

4.4.1 Where NordTech AB has funded a certification with a total cost exceeding **2,500 SEK**, and the employee voluntarily resigns within **12 months** of the certification being awarded, the following repayment schedule applies:

| Departure timing after certification | Repayment |
|---|---|
| Within 6 months | 100% of funded amount |
| 6–12 months | 50% of funded amount |
| After 12 months | No repayment required |

4.4.2 Repayment will be deducted from the employee's final salary payment, or invoiced if the final salary is insufficient to cover the amount owed.

4.4.3 This clause does not apply where employment is terminated by NordTech AB by reason of redundancy or dismissal without cause.

## 5. Performance and Development Reviews

5.1 All employees participate in NordTech AB's performance and development review cycle, which includes:
- A **mid-year check-in** (June) focused on progress against objectives and development needs.
- An **annual review** (December) covering performance, development achievements, and goal-setting for the coming year.

5.2 At each review, the employee and manager will agree on a minimum of **2 development goals** for the following period, documented in SmartHRIS.

5.3 Development goals may include targeted use of study leave, certification funding, on-the-job learning opportunities, mentoring, or internal mobility.
