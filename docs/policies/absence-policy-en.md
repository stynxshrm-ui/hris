# Absence and Leave Policy
## NordTech AB
**Version:** 1.4
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2026
**Approved by:** People & Culture, Executive Leadership Team
**Next Review:** January 2027

---

## 1. Introduction

1.1 NordTech AB is committed to supporting employees through periods of absence, illness, and life events. This policy sets out the entitlements and procedures for all types of absence and leave.

1.2 This policy applies to all employees of NordTech AB regardless of seniority, contract type, or length of service, except where a specific section states otherwise.

1.3 Managers are responsible for ensuring consistent application of this policy within their teams. People & Culture is available for guidance on any matters arising under this policy.

1.4 This policy should be read in conjunction with the NordTech AB Employee Handbook and any applicable Swedish legislation, including the Swedish Social Insurance Code (*Socialförsäkringsbalken*, SFB) and the Swedish Parental Leave Act (*Föräldraledighetslagen*, SFS 1995:584).

## 2. Sick Leave

### 2.1 Notification Procedure

2.1.1 If an employee is unable to attend work due to illness or injury, they must notify their line manager **before the start of their normal working hours** on the first day of absence, or as early as practically possible.

2.1.2 Notification must be made by telephone or video call directly to the line manager. Notification by text message, email, or via a third party is not acceptable as the sole means of notification unless exceptional circumstances prevent direct contact.

2.1.3 If the absence continues, the employee must update their manager daily unless a different arrangement has been agreed in writing.

### 2.2 Qualifying Day and Sick Pay

2.2.1 In line with Swedish law, the **first day of any period of sickness absence is an unpaid qualifying day** (*karensdag*). From the second consecutive day, statutory sick pay (*sjuklön*) is paid by the employer.

2.2.2 NordTech AB pays **sick pay at 80% of the employee's normal daily salary** from day 2 through day 14 of any continuous period of illness, in accordance with the Sick Pay Act (*Sjuklönelagen*, SFS 1991:1047).

2.2.3 From day 15 onwards, the employee may be eligible for sickness benefit (*sjukpenning*) from the Swedish Social Insurance Agency (*Försäkringskassan*). Employees must register their absence with Försäkringskassan directly.

2.2.4 NordTech AB supplements statutory sickness benefit for long-term absences, as specified in the applicable collective agreement or individual employment contract.

### 2.3 Medical Certificate

2.3.1 Employees must provide a medical certificate (*läkarintyg*) from a licensed physician if the absence extends to **8 consecutive calendar days** or more.

2.3.2 The medical certificate must be submitted to People & Culture within 2 working days of the certificate being issued.

2.3.3 NordTech AB reserves the right to request a medical certificate from day 1 of absence if there is a pattern of frequent short-term absences or other legitimate reason, with prior written notice to the employee.

### 2.4 Return to Work

2.4.1 Following any absence of **5 or more consecutive working days**, the employee must participate in a return-to-work conversation with their line manager before resuming full duties.

2.4.2 The return-to-work conversation is a supportive discussion, not a disciplinary meeting. Its purpose is to understand the nature of the illness, assess fitness to return, and identify any reasonable adjustments required.

2.4.3 Following long-term absence (more than 4 weeks), People & Culture will work with the employee and their manager to develop a phased return-to-work plan where appropriate.

### 2.5 Absence Monitoring

2.5.1 NordTech AB monitors absence levels for all employees. Absence data is recorded in SmartHRIS.

2.5.2 Where absence levels are causing concern, the line manager, in consultation with People & Culture, will initiate a formal absence review meeting with the employee. Persistently high levels of absence may, after full investigation and support, lead to formal action under the company's performance or conduct procedures.

## 3. Parental Leave

### 3.1 Entitlement

3.1.1 NordTech AB supports all employees' rights to parental leave in full accordance with the Swedish Parental Leave Act (*Föräldraledighetslagen*, SFS 1995:584).

3.1.2 Parents are entitled to a combined total of **480 days of parental leave per child**, paid through the Swedish Social Insurance Agency (*Försäkringskassan*). Of these:
- **390 days** are paid at 77.6% of the employee's qualifying income (up to the income ceiling set by *Försäkringskassan*).
- **90 days** are paid at the flat rate of 180 SEK per day.

3.1.3 Each parent is individually entitled to **240 days** of the total 480 days. **90 of these days are reserved for each parent and cannot be transferred to the other parent** (the so-called "reserved days" or *pappamånader/mammamånader*).

3.1.4 The remaining 180 days per parent may be transferred in whole or in part to the other parent.

3.1.5 Parental leave days may be taken at any time until the child reaches the age of **8 years**, or until the child completes their first year of school, whichever is later.

### 3.2 NordTech AB Parental Leave Enhancement

3.2.1 NordTech AB provides a **parental leave top-up payment** for eligible employees who have completed their probationary period. This supplements the statutory parental benefit to bring the employee's total income to **90% of their normal monthly salary** for up to **16 weeks** per child.

3.2.2 To qualify for the NordTech AB top-up, the employee must have been continuously employed for at least **6 months** at the time the leave commences.

3.2.3 The top-up is paid via payroll on receipt of confirmation of statutory parental benefit from *Försäkringskassan*.

### 3.3 Notification

3.3.1 Employees planning to take parental leave must notify People & Culture and their line manager in writing as early as possible, and in any event **no later than:**
- **3 months before** the intended start date for leave of 4 weeks or more.
- **6 weeks before** the intended start date for leave of less than 4 weeks.

3.3.2 Notice should be submitted via SmartHRIS and must include the intended start date, duration, and pattern of leave (full-time, part-time, etc.).

### 3.4 Rights During and After Parental Leave

3.4.1 During parental leave, the employee continues to accrue annual leave entitlement in full.

3.4.2 On return from parental leave, the employee has the right to return to the same or an equivalent role. People & Culture will conduct a reintegration meeting to support the transition back to work.

3.4.3 Employees may not be dismissed, made redundant, or disadvantaged in any way due to taking or planning to take parental leave.

## 4. Bereavement Leave

### 4.1 Entitlement

4.1.1 NordTech AB recognises that the death of a loved one is one of the most difficult experiences an employee can face. Employees are entitled to **5 days of paid bereavement leave** upon the death of an immediate family member.

4.1.2 For the purposes of this policy, **immediate family members** include:
- Spouse, civil partner, or domestic partner
- Child (including stepchildren and legally adopted children)
- Parent (including stepparents)
- Sibling (including step-siblings)
- Parent-in-law

4.1.3 Employees may be entitled to **up to 2 days of paid bereavement leave** for the death of an extended family member, including grandparents, grandchildren, aunts, uncles, or close friends, at the discretion of the line manager and People & Culture.

### 4.2 Taking Bereavement Leave

4.2.1 Bereavement leave should normally be taken within 2 weeks of the bereavement. However, NordTech AB recognises that grief is not linear, and flexibility will be shown where possible, for example where a funeral or memorial service is delayed.

4.2.2 Employees do not need to provide documentary evidence of the bereavement, although they may be asked to confirm the relationship to the deceased.

4.2.3 If an employee requires additional time beyond the entitlement above, they may request further time off as annual leave, unpaid leave, or through a flexible working arrangement with their manager's approval.

### 4.3 Ongoing Support

4.3.1 NordTech AB's Employee Assistance Programme (EAP) provides confidential counselling support to all employees dealing with bereavement. Details are available on the company intranet.

## 5. Unauthorised Absence

### 5.1 Definition

5.1.1 An absence is considered **unauthorised** where an employee:
- Fails to attend work without notification to their line manager.
- Provides false, misleading, or insufficient reasons for absence.
- Fails to follow the absence notification procedure set out in this policy.
- Does not return to work on the agreed date following a period of approved leave.
- Remains absent beyond the expiry of an approved leave period without authorisation.

### 5.2 Initial Steps

5.2.1 Where an employee fails to attend work without notification, the line manager must attempt to make contact with the employee within **2 hours** of the expected start time, using all reasonable means (phone, email, emergency contact).

5.2.2 If contact cannot be established after **24 hours**, People & Culture must be notified and the absence recorded as unauthorised in SmartHRIS.

### 5.3 Consequences

5.3.1 **Unauthorised absence is treated as a serious matter** and may constitute misconduct or gross misconduct depending on the circumstances.

5.3.2 Consequences may include one or more of the following, applied progressively and following investigation:

| Absence Duration | Minimum Action |
|---|---|
| 1 day, first occurrence | Return-to-work interview; informal counselling note |
| 1–3 days, repeat occurrence | Written warning; no pay for absence period |
| 3–5 days | Formal disciplinary hearing; final written warning; no pay for absence period |
| More than 5 days, or abandonment of employment | Disciplinary hearing; potential dismissal; no pay for absence period |

5.3.3 Salary will not be paid for any period of unauthorised absence. Deductions will be made from the next available payroll run.

5.3.4 Where the unauthorised absence is related to an underlying welfare issue, NordTech AB will seek to understand the circumstances before taking formal action, and may refer the employee to occupational health or the EAP.

5.3.5 Nothing in this policy prevents NordTech AB from treating a prolonged period of unauthorised absence as **abandonment of employment**, where it is reasonable to conclude that the employee does not intend to return. In such cases, a formal process will be followed before any decision is made.

## 6. Other Types of Leave

6.1 NordTech AB recognises other types of leave not covered in this policy, including:
- Emergency dependant leave
- Jury service
- Military service (*värnpliktsutbildning*)
- Study leave (see the Learning and Development Policy)

6.2 Entitlements and procedures for these types of leave are detailed in the relevant supplementary policies available on the company intranet.
