## NordTech AB
**Version:** 2.1
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2026
**Approved by:** People & Culture, Executive Leadership Team

## 1. Introduction

1.1 This Employee Handbook sets out the terms and conditions of employment at NordTech AB, a technology company headquartered in Stockholm, Sweden. It applies to all employees regardless of role, location, or employment type unless a specific section states otherwise.

1.2 This handbook supplements your individual employment contract. In the event of any conflict between this handbook and your contract, the terms of your contract shall prevail.

1.3 NordTech AB reserves the right to update this handbook at any time. Employees will be notified of material changes with a minimum of 30 days' notice. The current version is always available on the company intranet.

## 2. Holiday Entitlement

### 2.1 Annual Leave Allowance

2.1.1 All full-time employees are entitled to **25 days of paid annual leave** per calendar year, in addition to Swedish public holidays.

2.1.2 Part-time employees receive a pro-rated holiday entitlement calculated on the basis of their contracted hours relative to a standard 40-hour working week.

2.1.3 The holiday year runs from **1 January to 31 December**.

2.1.4 New employees joining part-way through the holiday year will receive a pro-rated entitlement for the remainder of that year, calculated from their start date.

### 2.2 Holiday Booking

2.2.1 Holiday requests must be submitted via the company HR system (SmartHRIS) with a minimum of:
- Two weeks' notice for absences of up to five consecutive days.
- Four weeks' notice for absences of six or more consecutive days.

2.2.2 Approval is at the discretion of the employee's line manager, taking into account business needs and team coverage.

2.2.3 Employees are encouraged to take at least two consecutive weeks of holiday per year in accordance with the Swedish Annual Leave Act (*Semesterlagen*, SFS 1977:480).

### 2.3 Holiday Carry-Over

2.3.1 Employees may carry over a maximum of **5 days** of unused annual leave from one holiday year to the next.

2.3.2 Carried-over leave must be used by **31 March** of the following year. Any carried-over leave not taken by this date will be forfeited without compensation unless the failure to take leave was directly caused by company-mandated restrictions on holiday periods.

2.3.3 Employees wishing to carry over leave must notify their line manager and People & Culture by **1 December** of the current holiday year.

2.3.4 Carry-over may not be accumulated across multiple years. Only leave from the immediately preceding year may be carried over.

### 2.4 Holiday Pay

2.4.1 Holiday pay is calculated and paid in accordance with the Swedish Annual Leave Act. For salaried employees, holiday pay is equivalent to regular salary plus a holiday supplement of 0.43% of monthly salary per holiday day taken.

2.4.2 On termination of employment, any accrued but untaken holiday will be paid out. Any holiday taken in excess of entitlement will be deducted from the final salary payment.

## 3. Notice Periods

### 3.1 Employee Notice

The notice period required from an employee who wishes to resign is determined by their length of continuous service:

| Length of Service | Notice Period |
|---|---|
| Probation period | 1 month |
| Up to 2 years | 1 month |
| 2–4 years | 2 months |
| 4–6 years | 3 months |
| 6–8 years | 4 months |
| 8–10 years | 5 months |
| 10 years or more | 6 months |

3.1.1 Notice must be submitted in writing to the employee's line manager and People & Culture.

### 3.2 Employer Notice

3.2.1 NordTech AB will provide a minimum notice period in accordance with the Swedish Employment Protection Act (*Lag om anställningsskydd*, LAS, SFS 1982:80). Employer notice periods are:

| Length of Service | Minimum Notice Period |
|---|---|
| Less than 2 years | 1 month |
| 2–4 years | 2 months |
| 4–6 years | 3 months |
| 6–8 years | 4 months |
| 8–10 years | 5 months |
| 10 years or more | 6 months |

3.2.2 In cases of summary dismissal for gross misconduct, no notice period applies. This is subject to a formal investigation and approval by People & Culture and the Chief People Officer.

### 3.3 Garden Leave

3.3.1 NordTech AB reserves the right to place an employee on garden leave during their notice period. During garden leave, the employee remains employed, continues to receive full pay and benefits, but is not required to attend the workplace or perform their duties.

3.3.2 Garden leave may be applied where it is in the legitimate business interest of NordTech AB, for example to protect confidential information or client relationships.

## 4. Probationary Period

### 4.1 Duration and Purpose

4.1.1 All new employees are subject to a probationary period of **6 months** from their start date.

4.1.2 The purpose of the probationary period is to allow both the employee and NordTech AB to assess suitability for the role and mutual fit with the company's culture and values.

### 4.2 Reviews During Probation

4.2.1 Line managers will conduct formal probation reviews at **month 1, month 3, and month 6**.

4.2.2 Review outcomes will be documented in SmartHRIS and shared with the employee.

4.2.3 If concerns arise about performance or conduct during probation, People & Culture must be notified immediately. A performance improvement plan may be put in place.

### 4.3 Extension of Probation

4.3.1 The probationary period may be extended by up to **3 additional months** at the discretion of the line manager and People & Culture where performance concerns have been identified and further assessment is required.

4.3.2 The employee must be informed of any extension in writing, with reasons provided, no later than **2 weeks before** the original probation end date.

### 4.4 Holiday During Probation

4.4.1 Employees continue to accrue annual leave during their probationary period at the standard rate.

4.4.2 However, **no carry-over of annual leave is permitted during or at the end of the probationary period**. Any unused leave accrued during probation that has not been taken by the probation end date will be forfeited, unless the employee has been unable to take leave due to company-imposed restrictions.

4.4.3 Holiday requests during probation are subject to the same approval process as for all employees, but line managers may reasonably request that non-urgent leave is deferred until after probation is completed.

### 4.5 Termination During Probation

4.5.1 During the probationary period, either party may terminate employment with 1 month's written notice.

4.5.2 NordTech AB may terminate employment with immediate effect during probation only in cases of serious misconduct.

## 5. Flexible Working Policy

### 5.1 Principles

5.1.1 NordTech AB is committed to enabling a flexible, outcomes-focused working environment. We believe that flexibility supports employee wellbeing, productivity, and retention.

5.1.2 This policy applies to all employees who have successfully completed their probationary period. Employees on probation may work flexibly at their manager's discretion, but have no formal right to request flexible working under this policy.

### 5.2 Hybrid Working

5.2.1 The default working arrangement at NordTech AB is **hybrid working**, with employees expected to attend the office a minimum of **2 days per week**.

5.2.2 Teams may agree on specific anchor days for in-office attendance to facilitate collaboration. These anchor days are set by the team lead in consultation with the team.

5.2.3 Employees are expected to be reachable and responsive during core hours of **09:00–15:00 CET** on all working days, whether working from the office or remotely.

### 5.3 Flexible Hours

5.3.1 Outside of core hours (09:00–15:00 CET), employees have flexibility to structure their remaining working hours as suits their individual needs and role requirements, subject to business needs.

5.3.2 Any arrangement to regularly start before 07:00 or finish after 20:00 must be agreed in writing with the line manager.

### 5.4 Formal Flexible Working Requests

5.4.1 Employees wishing to make a permanent or long-term change to their working pattern (e.g., reduced hours, compressed working week, fully remote working) should submit a formal flexible working request via SmartHRIS.

5.4.2 NordTech AB will consider all requests seriously and respond within **6 weeks**. A request may only be refused on one or more of the following grounds:
- Burden of additional costs
- Detrimental effect on ability to meet customer demand
- Inability to reorganise work among existing staff
- Detrimental impact on quality or performance
- Insufficient work available during the proposed working hours

5.4.3 Where a request is refused, written reasons will be provided and the employee has the right to appeal the decision within 10 working days.

### 5.5 Equipment and Expenses

5.5.1 NordTech AB will provide standard equipment (laptop, headset, peripherals) for remote working. Employees are responsible for maintaining a safe and productive home working environment.

5.5.2 A one-time home office setup allowance of up to **3,000 SEK** is available to all permanent employees after completing their probationary period, subject to manager approval and submission of receipts.
